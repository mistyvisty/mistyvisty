#### 🩺 PCOS × Neurodivergence — Advanced RAG (Pinecone + Hybrid Search)
> `Pinecone` `BM25` `RRF` `CrossEncoder` `Query Expansion` `MiniLM`

Upgraded RAG pipeline with Pinecone, BM25 sparse retrieval, Reciprocal Rank Fusion, CrossEncoder reranking and LLM query expansion. A/B tested against a FAISS baseline on the same 10 questions, same chunks and same LLM, so only the retrieval stack changed.

**Result: On a small, clean corpus, the hybrid stack matched dense-only retrieval on 7/10 questions at ~1.6× latency — and exposed a metadata-retrieval failure both pipelines shared**

#### 🤖 Fine-Tuned Phi-3 on PCOS Data — QLoRA
> `QLoRA` `PEFT` `Unsloth` `Transformers` `TRL` `Google Colab T4`

Fine-tuned Phi-3-mini (3.8B) with 4-bit quantisation and LoRA (r=16, α=16, all attention + MLP projections) on 15 hand-curated PCOS Q&A pairs — training 0.78% of parameters on a free T4 GPU. Compared against Llama 3.1 8B and documented the evaluation's flaws (leakage, baseline choice, keyword metric).

**Result: 123.7 MB LoRA adapter · Llama 3.1 8B still scored higher (28.9% vs 22.9%) — showing 15 examples shape format, not knowledge, and that RAG beats fine-tuning for factual medical Q&A**

🔗 [Repo](https://github.com/mistyvisty/pcos-finetune-phi3-lora)

#### 🛒 Catalog Image Quality Gate — CNN + Transfer Learning
> `PyTorch` `ResNet-18` `Grad-CAM` `Google Colab T4`

Image-quality classifier for e-commerce product photos. Built a low-quality class by synthetically degrading Myntra catalog images (blur, low brightness, low contrast), split by source image so no image appears in both train and validation. Compared a custom 3-layer CNN with frozen ResNet-18 transfer learning and inspected predictions with Grad-CAM.

**Result: CNN 99.1% · ResNet-18 99.9% val accuracy — high because synthetic degradations are easy to detect; robustness to real-world photos is the documented next step**

🔗 [Repo](https://github.com/mistyvisty/amazon-catalog-quality-gate)

### 📊 Data Science & Machine Learning

---

#### 🩺 PCOS Detection & Risk Prediction
> `Random Forest` `XGBoost` `SHAP` `SMOTE` `ImbPipeline` `5-Fold Stratified CV`

ML pipeline on 541 patients from 10 hospitals. Removed physiologically impossible hormone values with documented reference ranges, engineered `Total_follicles` (became the #1 SHAP predictor), and ran leakage-free 5-fold CV with SMOTE inside the pipeline. Tuned the threshold for screening, where missing a PCOS case is costlier than a false alarm.

**Result: CV AUC 0.946–0.957 (RF / XGBoost) · held-out AUC 0.912 · recall raised from 83% to 91% at the cost of precision (78% → 52%)**

🔗 [Repo](https://github.com/mistyvisty/PCOS_Detection_Medical)
📝 [Why I Built a PCOS Prediction Model — And Why It Was Never Just a Portfolio Project](https://medium.com/@bhardwajpreeti357/why-i-built-a-pcos-prediction-model-and-why-it-was-never-just-a-portfolio-project-85f64126ed2f)

---

#### 🏦 Bank Customer Churn Prediction
> `XGBoost` `SMOTE` `ImbPipeline` `5-Fold Stratified CV` `Data Leakage`

Caught a target-leaking feature: `Complain` matched the churn label for 99.86% of customers because complaints are recorded as customers leave, so it's unavailable at prediction time. Removed it, then compared three models with leakage-free SMOTE cross-validation and turned EDA into segment-level retention recommendations.

**Result: XGBoost test AUC 0.847 (CV 0.841 ± 0.010) · Germany churns at 2× other markets · customers with 2 products churn at 7.6% vs 27.7% with 1**

🔗 [Repo](https://github.com/mistyvisty/Bank-Churn-Prediction)
